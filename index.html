03 · HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. No broken paper slots.

1. Choose your PDFs and fill in the board, level, subject, syllabus code and year.

2. Click Add selected PDFs. The site saves the files in your current browser and extracts searchable text from each PDF.

3. Use AI Search to find a paper by metadata or a phrase inside its questions. Click a result to open that paper in the viewer.

Scanned PDFs that contain only images may not have searchable text unless OCR has been applied first.
